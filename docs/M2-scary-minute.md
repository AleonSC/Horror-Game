# M2: the player and the scary minute

Goal: one minute with one scour that's genuinely tense. Everything later builds on it, so we iterate here
until it is.

## 1. Pull and build the scene

1. GitHub Desktop: **Fetch origin**, then **Pull origin**. Open the project and check the Console for red errors.
2. Top menu: **Game > Build Scary Minute Scene**. It opens `Assets/Scenes/Generated/ScaryMinute/ScaryMinute.unity`.
3. Press **Play** and click in the Game view to capture the mouse.

## 2. Controls

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / mouse | walk / look |
| Shift | run (stamina: the thin bar bottom left) |
| Ctrl or C | crouch |
| Space | jump |
| F | flashlight on/off (battery: the bar bottom left) |
| Left click | use what the crosshair is on (crank the lamp) |
| Esc | pause menu (it frees the mouse) |
| F9 / F11 | dev window / session log |

## 3. What happens

- **A calm minute.** The first scour appears after 60 seconds (Assets/Config/Entities/ScourConfig, "First Delay").
  To skip the wait: F9 > Game > **Make one appear now**.
- **The scour** appears 22-32 m ahead. Half of them peek out from behind a trunk and duck back when you look.
  - **Look away** and it creeps 3 m closer every 3-6 seconds. **With your light off, it creeps even while you watch it.**
    A creep that would bring it within 3 m takes you.
  - **Some creeps take it behind a tree** on its way to you (35%): it hides behind the trunk and peeks out when you look away.
  - **Get near it in the dark** (within 14 m, the light not on it) and it grows and comes for you, with a pounding beat.
  - **Face it down**: walk at it with the beam on it. Its eyes turn blue, you slow to a stop 2.5 m away, it
    shudders to a crescendo, then a black hole opens on the ground under it, dark motes swirling round it, and it
    falls in. That costs 10% battery. It's back 7-12 s later.
  - **Flash the light on and off at it 4 times quickly** and it shrieks and charges your face, then vanishes.
    It comes back sooner and angrier.
  - **Taken**: you're held, it walks up to you, a hole tears open behind it, it drags you through, and the
    screen fades. You wake up (groggy) by the last lamp you lit, or where you started.
- **The lamp** is in the clearing up the path. Click it repeatedly to crank its meter. Stop and the meter drains.
  When it's full it lights: a 13 m safe circle the scour can't enter (it prowls the edge instead).
- **Sound states.** With the scour within 7 m, the world goes quiet and you hear breathing (Dread). When it's
  close enough to take you, it's just your heartbeat (Hush).

## 4. What to tell me

- Is it tense? Which parts work, and which feel flat or unfair?
- What's too fast or too slow: creeping, advancing, facing it down, the take?
- Anything that looks or sounds wrong. Screenshots and the session log (F11 > Copy) help a lot.

All the numbers are in `Assets/Config/Entities/ScourConfig`, `Player/PlayerConfig`, `Player/FlashlightConfig` and
`World/LampConfig`. Changes made in Play mode are kept. Push them, or tell me what you changed.

## Stand-ins (on purpose)

- **The scour** is now built from the plan's description: a jointed, too-thin figure with vertebrae, ribs, a
  small tall head on a long neck, backward-bending elbows, long curling fingers, and shins that thin away
  above the ground. A quarter have extra arms from the ribs. Its jaw drops open as it nears, with a glowing
  throat, fangs and a red glare on the ground. Each creep shows a new pose, its legs trail as it moves, and it
  twitches. (No walking animation: it moves when unseen, or glides.)
- **All the sounds** are generated by code and are first drafts. Recorded sounds can replace any of them.
- **The HUD** is a plain first version. The polished one comes in M7.
